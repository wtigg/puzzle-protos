SHOVE OFF!

Push'em All as a grid puzzle. Two versions to compare: pick one.

VERSION A
Water
Swipe a row or column: the roller shoves everyone in its way off the edge into the water. Fences stop the heap, pillars split it in two.
 VERSION B
Hole
Fenced platform with a round black hole. Shove the crowd across the hole to feed it. It grows as it eats, swallowing crowds and pillars in its way.
15 levels each. While you drag, a preview shows where everyone ends up, and Undo.
Portrait, touch or mouse. Works offline.
