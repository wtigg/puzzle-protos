FLIP & STICK

Slice It All as a puzzle. Same levels, two controls: pick one.

VERSION A
Tap to land
Tap where the knife should land: it flips there at once. Every fruit under its arc is sliced. Hold your finger down to see the arc first.
 VERSION B
Hold, aim, release
Press a landing spot: the 3D arc and every fruit it will slice light up. Slide to another spot, release to flip.
15 levels each, no timing. Undo anytime.
Portrait, touch or mouse. Works offline.
